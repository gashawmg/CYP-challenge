What the pipeline actually does

Two independent stages. The model decides the order; placement decides where the numbers sit. They are scored by different parts of the same metric.

1 · The multi-head model

Thirteen assays, one shared encoder, and only four outputs ever leave the building.

2 · Why we place instead of submitting raw predictions

Because the metric charges two things at once, and the model only controls one of them.

ST-RAE charges absolute distance from the measured interval. A perfect ordering placed 1.4 log units too high scores badly; that is exactly what CYP2D6 did at 1.3092 before placement, and 0.6602 after — same model, same ordering.
